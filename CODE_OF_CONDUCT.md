# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

RobotStudio is intended to support learning, experimentation, and respectful
technical discussion around programming and robotics.

This project is shared for personal study use only. Participation in discussions
or contributions does not grant commercial, organizational, institutional,
brand-related, redistribution, sublicensing, or trademark rights.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and constructive;
- focus feedback on ideas, code, tests, and documentation;
- explain technical disagreements clearly and calmly;
- welcome learners and beginner questions;
- avoid harassment, insults, intimidation, or personal attacks;
- respect the project license and ownership.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, discrimination, threats, or abusive language;
- sexualized, violent, or demeaning comments;
- publishing private information without permission;
- using the project, its name, or its materials in ways that violate the license;
- misrepresenting affiliation with the project or its owner;
- pressuring maintainers to approve commercial, organizational, or branded use.

## Enforcement

The project owner may remove comments, issues, pull requests, discussions, or
contributions that violate this code of conduct.

The project owner may also block or restrict participation from people who act
against the learning-focused purpose of the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner through the contact
channel listed in the repository, if one is available. Do not publicly disclose
sensitive personal information when reporting an issue.
